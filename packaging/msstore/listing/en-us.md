## Description

```
FishTorrnet is a free, open source BitTorrent client for people who are tired of ads and clutter.

Watch while it downloads. Videos and music start playing right away in the built-in player, because the pieces you need are fetched first.

FishGuard checks every torrent before it starts. It catches fake movies with files like video.mp4.exe, hidden characters that flip file names, programs and fake codecs inside movie torrents, ad shortcuts and password files.

Control from your phone. Turn on remote access, scan the QR code and manage downloads from any phone on your Wi-Fi.

Move in with one click. Import your torrents from qBittorrent and uTorrent together with their folders, and keep seeding.

Keeps working in the background. Close the window and FishTorrnet stays in the tray, downloading.

Everything you expect from a BitTorrent client: magnet links, .torrent files, trackers, DHT, PEX, uTP, UPnP, speed limits, file selection. Dark and light themes, 10 languages.

No ads, no telemetry, no accounts. Ever.

FishTorrnet is a general purpose file sharing tool. Download only content you have the right to download.
```

## What's new in this version
