## Fixes

- **Linux:** FishTorrnet no longer crashes at startup when the system has no tray library, for example on Fedora or KDE without app indicator support. The app starts without a tray icon, and the close button quits it. Install `libayatana-appindicator` to get the icon.
- The `.deb` and `.rpm` packages now recommend the tray library, so most systems install it automatically.
- Windows: "Apps & features" now shows the publisher as FishTorrnet contributors.

## First launch

FishTorrnet is free, so the installers are not signed with a paid certificate, and your system will warn you once.

- **Windows:** click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
- **macOS:** open **System Settings → Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway**.

To check a download, run `sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS.txt --ignore-missing` in the folder with the file.
